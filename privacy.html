📋 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how AstroSling ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you play our game. This policy applies to the iOS application available on the Apple App Store.

In plain English: AstroSling is a free, ad-supported arcade game. We do not collect your personal data ourselves. The App contains advertising powered by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device identifiers to serve ads — but only if you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can also remove ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase processed securely by Apple.

📊 2. Data We Collect

AstroSling itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics of our own. The game runs entirely on your device.

However, the App integrates third-party services that may collect data as described in sections 4, 5, and 6 below:

  • Google AdMob — may collect device identifiers and usage data for advertising purposes (only with your consent via ATT).
  • Apple StoreKit — handles in-app purchase transactions; Apple processes your payment information.

📱 3. Data Stored On Your Device

AstroSling stores a small amount of game state exclusively on your device using iOS local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

What is stored locally:

  • Levels unlocked and completed
  • High scores and star ratings
  • Speed Run personal best times
  • Daily Challenge completion status
  • Unlocked ship skins
  • Sound and music preferences (on/off)
  • "Remove Ads" purchase status

This data is tied to your device and iCloud backup settings. Deleting the app removes all local game data.

📢 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

AstroSling displays interstitial ads (full-screen ads shown between levels) powered by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC.

What AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA) — a resettable device identifier used to serve personalized ads and measure ad performance. Collected only if you grant ATT permission (see Section 5).
  • Coarse location — approximate location derived from IP address, used for regional ad targeting.
  • Interaction data — whether you viewed or tapped an ad.
  • Diagnostics — basic app crash and performance data.

⚠️ If you deny the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, AdMob will not access your IDFA and will serve non-personalized ads only. Your choice does not affect gameplay.

Ads are shown between levels, approximately every 3 levels. Ads are never shown during active gameplay.

You can remove all ads permanently with the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase described in Section 6. Users who purchase "Remove Ads" are never shown ads, and AdMob is not initialized for those users.

For more information about Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

🔒 5. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

Apple requires apps to ask your permission before accessing your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for cross-app tracking. When you first launch AstroSling, iOS will display Apple's standard ATT permission prompt.

If you tap "Allow": AdMob may use your IDFA to show personalized ads and measure their effectiveness across apps.

If you tap "Ask App Not to Track": Your IDFA is not accessed. AdMob will only show non-personalized (contextual) ads. Gameplay is completely unaffected.

You can change your ATT choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → AstroSling.

💳 6. In-App Purchases

AstroSling offers one optional in-app purchase:

Remove Ads — a one-time, non-consumable purchase that permanently removes all advertisements from the game.

All payments are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, store, or process your payment card details. Apple's payment processing is governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

The "Remove Ads" status is stored locally on your device. You can restore your purchase on a new device using the "Restore Purchases" button in the game's About screen.

For questions about billing, contact Apple Support or visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

👶 7. Children's Privacy

AstroSling is rated 4+ on the Apple App Store and is suitable for all ages.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The App itself collects no personal data from any user. AdMob, when initialized, applies additional protections for users who may be minors in accordance with Google's policies and applicable law (COPPA, GDPR-K).

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us at arche.boost@gmail.com so we can take prompt corrective action.

⚖️ 8. Your Privacy Rights

Since AstroSling itself does not collect or store personal data on our servers, most traditional data access/deletion requests are not applicable to us directly.

However, if you are in the EU (GDPR), California (CCPA), or another jurisdiction with applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalized ads — deny or revoke ATT permission (Section 5)
  • Delete local game data — delete the App from your device
  • Access or deletion requests for AdMob data — contact Google via their privacy tools at safety.google/privacy
  • Contact us for any other privacy questions: arche.boost@gmail.com

🛡️ 10. Security

Since AstroSling does not transmit personal data to our own servers, there is minimal attack surface for data breaches affecting our users. Game progress is stored locally on your device, protected by iOS's standard app sandbox security.

Third-party SDKs (AdMob, Apple StoreKit) are maintained by their respective companies and follow industry security standards.
