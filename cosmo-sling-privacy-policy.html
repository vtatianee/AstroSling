Privacy Policy

Your Privacy is Sacred

Cosmo Sling is a single-player arcade game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being completely transparent about how the app works.

✓ No personal data collected

📋 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Cosmo Sling ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you play our game. This policy applies to the iOS application available on the Apple App Store.

Last updated: March 2026  ·  Effective date: March 2026

🛡️ 2. Data We Do NOT Collect

🔒
Cosmo Sling collects zero personal data.

We do not have servers. We do not have accounts. We do not track you.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Name, email address, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs (IDFA), or device fingerprints
  • Location data (GPS or approximate)
  • IP addresses or network information
  • Gameplay telemetry, session lengths, or analytics events
  • Crash reports (crashes are handled locally by iOS)
  • Camera, microphone, contacts, or any other device data
  • Payment information (the App is free with no in-app purchases)

📱 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Cosmo Sling stores a small amount of data exclusively on your device using the browser's localStorage (or equivalent native storage). This data never leaves your device.

Data Item Purpose Sent to server?
Best score (endless mode) Display your personal high score Never
Level progress Remember which levels you unlocked Never
Sound preferences Remember your music/SFX settings Never

💡 You can delete all locally stored data at any time by deleting the App from your device.

🔗 4. Third-Party Services

Cosmo Sling does not integrate with any third-party services, including:

  • No analytics (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.)
  • No advertising networks (no AdMob, Meta Ads, etc.)
  • No social login (no Facebook, Google, Apple Sign-In)
  • No cloud saves or leaderboard services (no Game Center, Play Games)
  • No crash reporting tools (no Sentry, Crashlytics, etc.)
  • No CDN or external font loading during gameplay

All game assets — graphics, music, and sound effects — are generated entirely on-device using the Web Audio API and HTML5 Canvas. No external network requests are made during gameplay.

🔐 5. App Permissions

Cosmo Sling requests no special device permissions. The App does not ask for access to:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Location services
  • Contacts or calendar
  • Photo library
  • Health or motion data
  • Bluetooth or NFC
  • Push notifications

Audio playback uses the standard iOS audio session and does not require any special permission.

👶 6. Children's Privacy

Cosmo Sling is rated 4+ on the Apple App Store and is suitable for all ages, including children under 13.

Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, we are fully compliant with:

  • COPPA – Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (United States)
  • GDPR-K – GDPR provisions for children (European Union)
  • Apple's Kids Category guidelines

No parental consent is required to play, as no personal information is collected, transmitted, or stored externally.

🔒 7. Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breach from our side. The only data that exists (your local scores and settings) is protected by the iOS sandbox — other apps cannot access it.

🌍 8. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Because Cosmo Sling does not collect personal data, most data rights requests are not applicable. However, if you are located in the European Union (GDPR) or California (CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access – Request confirmation of what data we hold (answer: none)
  • Deletion – Request deletion of your data (done by uninstalling the App)
  • Portability – Export your data (no data to export)
  • Opt-out – Opt out of sale of personal data (we sell nothing)

To exercise any of these rights, or if you have any questions, contact us at the address below.
